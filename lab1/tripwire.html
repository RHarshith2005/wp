Tripwire

About the Edition:

  • Originally published: June 1999
  • Author: Lee Child
  • Page count: 576
  • Genres: Mystery, Thriller, Crime Fiction

Description(Summary):

Tripwire is the third book in the Jack Reacher series by Lee Child. Reacher is living peacefully in Key West digging swimming pools by hand until a private investigator from New York shows up asking questions, pulling Reacher into a deadly confrontation with an enigmatic adversary from Vietnam.
