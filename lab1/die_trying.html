Die Trying

About the Edition:

  • Originally published: July 1998
  • Author: Lee Child
  • Page count: 560
  • Genres: Thriller, Fiction, Suspense
  • Publishers: G.P. Putnam's Sons, Bantam Books

Description(Summary):

Die Trying is the second novel in the Jack Reacher series written by Lee Child. In this electrifying thriller, Jack Reacher is kidnapped on a Chicago street along with a mysterious woman, and they must survive in a remote militant compound against dangerous odds.
