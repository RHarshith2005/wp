INTRODUCTION

The Heart of Karnataka

Bengaluru (formerly known as Bangalore) is the vibrant capital city of Karnataka, located in southern India on the Deccan Plateau.

Renowned as the "Garden City of India" for its historic boulevards and sprawling lung spaces like Cubbon Park and Lalbagh, Bengaluru has transformed into the premier "Silicon Valley of India", contributing over 35% of the nation's IT exports.

Despite its rapid modernization, Bengaluru retains its soulful charm: crisp mornings scented with filter coffee, majestic colonial and Dravidian architecture, thriving theater and music scenes, and a warm cosmopolitan population.

Did You Know?

Origin of the Name

According to popular legend, King Veera Ballala II of the Hoysala dynasty lost his way in the forest. An elderly woman offered him boiled beans (*Benda Kaalu*). Grateful, he named the settlement Benda-Kaalu-Uru ("Town of Boiled Beans"), which eventually evolved into Bengaluru.

"Bengaluru is not just a city; it is an emotion of pleasant breezes, ambitious dreams, and warm hospitality."

Landmarks That Define The Skyline

From monolithic neo-Dravidian government edifices to centuries-old royal botanical conservatories.

Governance & Heritage

Vidhana Soudha

Constructed in 1956 under Chief Minister Kengal Hanumanthaiah, this grand granite monument is the seat of Karnataka's state legislature. Combining Neo-Dravidian architectural craftsmanship with imposing granite pillars and four entrance porches, it stands illuminated with thousands of lights on festive evenings.

📍 Dr. Ambedkar Veedhi 🏛 Neo-Dravidian Granite
Botanical Wonder

Lalbagh Botanical Garden

Commissioned by Hyder Ali in 1760 and completed by Tipu Sultan, Lalbagh spans 240 acres harboring over 1,800 species of flora and centuries-old trees. At its core stands the iconic Victorian Glass House modeled after London's Crystal Palace, host to the bi-annual world-famous Republic Day Flower Show.

ECONOMIC ENGINE

The Silicon Valley of India

Bengaluru is globally celebrated as India's high-tech capital. It hosts the headquarters of technological titans such as Infosys and Wipro, the research labs of global giants (Google, Microsoft, Intel, Amazon, Apple, Cisco), and the nerve center of India's space program — ISRO.

  • ◆
    Global Aerospace & Research: Home to the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Raman Research Institute, and HAL.
  • ◆
    Unicorn Capital: Bengaluru is birthplace to India's largest tech startups including Flipkart, Swiggy, Zerodha, and Razorpay.
  • ◆
    Sustainable Innovation: Next-generation campus designs with lush pedestrian greenways, water lakes, and metro connectivity.
CULINARY HERITAGE

Flavors of Bengaluru: From Tiffin to Filter Coffee

Experience the mouth-watering vegetarian morning rituals and bustling Darshini cafes.

🥗

Benne Masala Dosa

Golden, crispy exterior roasted with aromatic butter (benne), wrapped around spiced potato mash, served alongside fresh coconut and tangy spicy tomato chutney.

☕

Authentic Filter Kaapi

Brewed with freshly ground chicory-infused coffee beans, mixed with piping hot frothy milk, and poured gracefully back and forth in traditional brass dabara and tumbler sets.

🍲

Thatte Idli & Medu Vada

Pillow-soft plate-sized steamed rice cakes (*Thatte Idli*) bathed in aromatic clarified butter (ghee) and gun-powder (*chutney podi*), paired with crunchy golden lentil donuts (*vada*).

🍴

Legendary Heritage Cafes

Mavalli Tiffin Room (MTR), Vidyarthi Bhavan in Gandhi Bazaar, Central Tiffin Room (CTR / Shri Sagar) in Malleshwaram, and Brahmins' Coffee Bar.
