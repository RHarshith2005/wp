It Starts with us


Book Details:

  • Author: Colleen Hoover
  • Original Language: English
  • Genre: Romance, Contemporary fiction
  • Publisher: Atria Books
  • Published Date: October 18, 2022
  • Main Characters: Lily Bloom, Atlas Corrigan, Ryle Kincaid
  • Page Count: 336 pages

Synopsis:

It Starts with Us is the direct sequel to the bestselling phenomenon It Ends with Us. Told from the alternating perspectives of Lily and Atlas, the story picks up right where the epilogue of the first book left off, revealing more about Atlas's past and following Lily as she embraces a second chance at true love.
